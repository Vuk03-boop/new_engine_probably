## 2. Corrections and qualifications that now govern our documents

### A09-01 — Correct the default-AO cost claim (substantive)

**Affected text:** our Batch 08 §3, “Existing lighting is not an NRC training pipeline,” cost accounting:

> Turning off a default-off AO pass saves nothing in the default configuration.

**This inference is incorrect.** “Effect off” was confused with “producer not executed.” The source chain is:

1. `src/config.rs::Config::default` sets `dir_ao: false` and `shadow_pass: true`.
2. `src/scene.rs::spec_hi_from` unconditionally includes `FLAG_HI_SHADOW_PASS`; this selects the split family in the ordinary path.
3. `src/render/mod.rs::Renderer::render_ready` dispatches `ao_probe` at half resolution inside that family, **without a `FLAG_DIR_AO` condition**. The renderer file contains that flag’s constant declaration, not a dispatch gate.
4. `src/render/shaders/ao.wgsl::ao_probe` does not check that flag. Valid guides reach **five `ao_tap` calls**. Invalid guides write neutral output and return.
5. `ao_sample` checks the flag and returns `1.0` before sampling when disabled. That removes the **shading contribution/sample**, not the earlier producer dispatch.
6. `src/render/timer.rs::Readback::family_timestamp` brackets the split family. AO has no dedicated timestamp pair; its work is inside the family’s `q::RESOLVE` interval. `total_ms` does not also add the three shadow subintervals, so do not diagnose pre-existing double-counting merely from their existence.

**Replacement statement:** directional AO’s contribution defaults off, but the current split path still dispatches its producer. Default baseline profiling must include this work. A separately authorized flag-gating experiment may avoid unnecessary production while preserving off-arm output; its actual savings, shader/API validity, texture initialization and toggle behavior remain unmeasured.

This also qualifies Batch 08 §6’s H12 cost discussion, “default where AO is off,” STATUS’s default-off descriptions, and any synthesis that infers zero cost from an inactive feature. They should be read as **appearance state only**. Their five-tap/half-resolution descriptions remain correct.

New diagnostic label **D-AO** is deliberately separate from H12’s neural-learning/matrix hypothesis. It does not approve NRC, a two-layer network, a <0.1 ms promise, or removing the probe field. No engine patch was made. Both RR-selected inspection views confirm the flag/producer distinction; GPU behavior is not observed here.

**Lesson for this review:** follow enablement through producer scheduling *and* consumption. Passing the previous CPU models did not catch this prose error; those models did not test this cost inference.

## 4. What agreement with the other AI does—and does not—buy

**Useful agreement:** the current history producer gap; distinction between camera-visible receiver retention and light visibility; no automatic LoD mapping from repeated face UVs; conditional cooperative matrices; learned radiance versus probe/AO targets; JFA versus attenuated multi-channel lighting; and smaller first experiments instead of a combined architecture.

**Different witnesses, not contradictions:** their earlier-hit example uses different distances; their JFA error has squared distances 73/81 while ours reproduces a 109/113 paper example; their relaxation tests use 50 small 2D cases while ours use 24 small 3D cases. None of those counts should be added and advertised as our replicated test coverage. Only our own eight suites were rerun here.

**Do not import:** their clean-engine/archive receipts, their local link validation, their unprovided script execution, or historical timings as new target measurements. The source-visible lack of a fresh history producer is strong evidence, but neither report proves that it caused an old +0.15/+0.23 ms result.

**Final judgment:** the other reports are useful and technically compatible cross-checks, not a certification. Our original verdicts survive the code audit, but our default-AO cost claim did not. The corrected synthesis explicitly carries that exception rather than claiming blanket agreement or blanket correctness.
