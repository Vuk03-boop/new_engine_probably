# 0008. Ray reconstruction material guides

Status: Accepted

## Context

Water reflections read poorly under `--dlss`. Three causes were measured. Render
resolution was the first and is fixed (`optimal_render_resolution` in
`render/mod.rs`). The third is that `dlss_prep` reprojects the water *surface*,
so reflected geometry is handed the motion of the water it sits on; Super
Resolution has no way to track a reflection that moves against its own surface.

DLSS Ray Reconstruction exists for exactly that: a specular guide carrying the
world-space distance from the primary vertex to the specular hit, which lets the
reconstructor follow reflected detail under motion. It wants material guides
rather than final colour — diffuse albedo, specular albedo, world normal, linear
roughness — and it replaces Super Resolution rather than stacking with it
(RR Integration Guide 3.2).

The second supposed cause does not survive measurement, and this ADR exists partly
to record that. `water_compose` mixes the reflection toward flat sky with
coefficient `ctx.roughness * 0.65`, and the plan read that as "up to 65% of the
detail discarded". `ctx.roughness` is `optical_roughness`, a sub-pixel wave
variance term that measures 0.0 across the near field and peaks near 0.036, so the
coefficient is under 0.03. A/B across six vantages puts the maximum channel
difference at 10/255. The line is very nearly a no-op.

## Decision

Write the four material guides from `resolve.wgsl` rather than from a pass of its
own, because `resolve` is the only place that has albedo, the lighting split and
the sky path together, and because `dlss_prep` is dispatched *after* the resolve
fork so anything it wrote would be clobbered. The call is `rr_store`, defined in
`dlss_rr.wgsl` with the storage bindings and in `dlss_rr_stub.wgsl` with an empty
body and none, chosen by `#[cfg]` in `render::shader_source`. The call sites in
`resolve.wgsl` are unconditional and identical in both configurations.

Give the flatten an explicit switch, `--water-rough-flatten` /
`--no-water-rough-flatten`, defaulting on, with `--dlss-rr` implying off. The
reason is that RR owns roughness filtering and a hand-mix running underneath it
would filter twice — not that the hand-mix was destroying detail, because it was
not.

Hand `WaterCtx.roughness` to RR as the guide's linear roughness. Whether that
mapping is right is unverified: the term was built as a variance measure, not as
a material property, and it reads near zero over most of the surface. The water
does look near-mirror, so zero may be the honest description.

**This does not supersede ADR 0002.** The plan assumed it would. ADR 0002's
decision is to preserve wave variance and compensate by blending the *shading
normal* toward the geometric normal as variance grows; that blend
(`nw = normalize(mix(nw, n, optical_roughness))` in `water_ctx`) is untouched
here, as is the `--water-mottle` pinning. Only the separate reflection mix gets a
switch. Superseding 0002 would have claimed a reversal that did not happen.

## Alternatives considered

- **An `override` to keep shader text byte-identical when the feature is off** —
  rejected: the override and the calls would sit in `resolve.wgsl` regardless, and
  a referenced storage binding forces a pipeline layout change whether or not the
  branch is taken. The stub gives the guarantee that actually matters (unchanged
  layout, unchanged output) and the base screenshot hash verifies it.
- **Writing the guides from `dlss_prep.wgsl`** — rejected: it has the vis key but
  no atlas sample and no lighting split, so it cannot produce albedo or roughness,
  and its dispatch order is wrong.
- **Stacking RR on Super Resolution** — not available. Guide 3.2: RR overrides SR,
  which is no longer executed. The two flags refuse each other.
- **Removing the flatten outright rather than gating it** — rejected: it is
  reachable presentation, the pre-RR look is worth keeping bit-exact, and with the
  measured coefficient there is nothing to gain by deleting it.

## Consequences

Shader text changes once, permanently, relative to the pre-RR tree, so the
substring and occurrence-count pins in `tests/` needed one reconciliation pass.
Two moved: `tests/sky_specular.rs` pinned the substring `spec_hi`, which the new
`spec_hit` trips, and `tests/cutout.rs` counted decoder sites without accounting
for `dlss_prep.wgsl` (a pre-existing miscount that only showed up once the suite
was run under a `dlss-prep`-implying feature).

With the feature off, and with `dlss-rr-prep` compiled in but unused, `--screenshot`
stays byte-identical to the pre-RR baseline `580d9fbba4919a0b61f1941fa9250a22`.

Stages 1 and 2 are not expected to improve the image on their own, and stage 3
will not recover reflection detail because none is being lost. The payoff, if
there is one, is reflection stability under motion, which needs hardware and a
judged A/B to confirm. `docs/dlss-open-questions.md` carries the open questions:
jittered vs unjittered matrices for the specular guide, the RR jitter sign, and
the roughness mapping above.
