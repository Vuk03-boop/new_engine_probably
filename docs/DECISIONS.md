## Decision discipline

Use [the ADR template](templates/ADR.md) when a choice changes lasting architecture, interface, ownership, default or persistence behavior. Record authority: user approval or an explicitly delegated decision within an approved task. A model cannot promote a proposal merely because it wrote a convincing explanation.

Accepted design decisions are superseded with a new record when changed; factual errors receive labeled corrections. Keep results and implementation status separate from the decision itself. Rejected experiments retain their stop reason and reopening condition so they are not rediscovered every session.

ADR-0001 (language/toolchain), ADR-0002 (save format), ADR-0003 (device budget, visibility buffers; Amendment 3, the emitter table in the publication set, under S-024), ADR-0004 (surface mesh layout), ADR-0005 (light-transport conventions, reference renderer, real-time sky; delegated in S-017; Amendment 3, emitters, under S-024) and ADR-0006 (guides and the temporal history contract; delegated in S-017; Amendment 2, the lights as a light jump, under S-024 in 4B) are the accepted engine ADRs. Verified engine commands are listed in [engine/README.md](../engine/README.md). Do not copy a historical old-engine ADR into an accepted new-engine decision without a new rationale and validation plan.
