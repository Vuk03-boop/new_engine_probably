# Change: Phase 3B — real-time sun and shadows

Status: **passed on the RTX 3050 (2026-09-24).**
Date and baseline: 2026-09-24, after 3A (no Git).
Authorization: S-017 (slices 3A–3G).

## Objective

The first real-time term of M3 ([Phase 3 proposal](2026-09-24-phase3-proposal.md) §3B): direct sun with real shadows, from the G-buffer, in ADR-0005 units, equal to the reference's sun term, and shown in the viewer at any time of day.

## What was built

- **`gpu::shade`** + `gpu/shaders/shade.slang`: a compute pass per pixel.
  - It rebuilds the surface point from depth (t = near / depth along the pixel-centre ray), snaps it onto its voxel face plane and offsets it 1/256 voxel (ADR-0005).
  - It picks a point on the sun disk from the pixel's PCG32 stream (the reference's stream for the same frame and seed) and casts one shadow ray.
  - It writes linear HDR radiance (RGBA32F, 16 B/px, `GpuTemporal`, 33 MB at 1080p). Pixels without a surface show the sun disk; the sky is 3C.
  - Reflection-checked layout; planted faults `flip_x` (x-face normals flipped) and `depth` (surface point 1% too far).
- **`light_common.slang`**: the RNG, samplers and atmosphere moved out of `reference.slang` into one include shared by both passes (no behaviour change: the 3A reference tests pass unchanged after the move).
- **`debug_view`**: a ninth view, `View::Light`, tone-maps the radiance (`Lighting::light_exposure`, ACES fit), bounds-checked (magenta without a bound buffer); `bind_radiance`; the target transition split out as `targets_to_read` (fragment + compute readers) with `DebugView::draw`. `record` keeps its old behaviour. The G-buffer view tests in `tests/raster.rs` and `tests/edit.rs` skip `View::Light` (not a G-buffer view; checked in `tests/shade.rs`).
- **Viewer:** the light view is the default (key 0; the M1 lit view moved to key 9). Time of day: `[` / `]` ±15 minutes, `T` runs the day (1 h per 4 s), `--hour H` (default 9). Exposure (display only): a sunlit white Lambertian surface facing the sun maps near 1, `-` / `=` shift it in quarter stops. A `shade` GPU pass in the timings and the JSON summary; the summary adds `hour`. The shade bindings are rebuilt on resize and after edits (new TLAS).

**Criterion 3 correction (declared before the first run, revised after it).** The first version required each fault to fail every arm. `flip_x` cannot: under the noon point sun the sun has no x component, so a flipped x-face normal still gets cos θ = 0, and the fault has no effect there (0–1 px). The criterion now requires each fault to fail at least half of the arms and to mismatch at least 10× the correct arms' total. The correct-arm limit (0.1%) is unchanged.

**Cost** (`shade_cost_at_1080p`, validation off, 40 reps each, medians in ms; the settings ran one after another, so differences of about 10% are within drift):

| greedy / chunk (default) | 0.330 / 0.528 | 0.253 / 0.411 | 0.296 / 0.353 |
| greedy / 2³ chunks | 0.283 / 0.482 | 0.217 / 0.368 | 0.262 / 0.309 |
| greedy / brick | 0.299 / 0.507 | 0.238 / 0.401 | 0.252 / 0.338 |
| none / chunk | 0.467 / 0.460 | 0.429 / 0.369 | 0.441 / 0.310 |

ADR-0003 Amendment 2 asked for this when secondary rays arrive. Shadow rays through the merged meshes cost up to ~13% more than through unmerged ones, while raster costs ~40% less; the total is similar, and every setting is far inside the 16.7 ms frame. No reason to reopen the granularity default.

## Observations

- **The shop fronts face north.** At the equinox at 45° N the sun stays on the south side all day, so direct sun never reaches them; in the 3B view they are black (`engine/results/shade_3b_sheet.png`: dawn, 8 h, noon, 15 h, dusk). That is correct for a direct-only term: the reference images show them lit by the sky and by bounce light, which 3C and 3F add. A season setting (declination) would let summer morning and evening sun reach them; it is a one-line option of `SunPath` if wanted.
- One scripted-run frame took 21.8 ms: frame 301, the resize (swapchain recreation), as in earlier runs.
- The 2E debt item "greedy meshes trace about 2× slower in validation-on runs" was re-measured here with validation off: shadow rays through greedy meshes cost 5–15% more, not 2×. The validation-on effect itself was not re-examined; it does not affect timing runs, which are made with validation off. The item is closed in NOW.
